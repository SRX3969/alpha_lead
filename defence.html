The 5-Day SSB Architecture

Detailed breakdown of Stage-1 Screening, Psychological Testing, GTO Ground Tasks, and Personal Interview.

← Swipe to explore Day 1 to Day 5 →
Day 1
Stage-1 Screening

OIR verbal/non-verbal testing, PPDT perception, individual story narration, and group consensus discussion.

Day 2
Psychology Battery

12 TAT stories, 60 Word Association words, 60 Situation Reactions, and comprehensive Self-Description profiling.

Day 3
GTO Outdoor — Part 1

Group Discussion, Group Planning Exercise (GPE), Progressive Group Task (PGT), and Snake Race teamwork.

Day 4
GTO Part 2 & Interview

Individual Obstacles, Command Task leadership, Lecturette poise, and deep Personal Interview with Board IO.

Day 5
Board Conference

Joint evaluation by President, IO, Psych and GTO, individual candidate appearance, and recommendations.
