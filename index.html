Institutional Positioning

Training the human side of performance.

Alpha Lead Academy is a leadership and capability development institution built to train the human side of performance. We prepare motivated youth for the Armed Forces, help corporate leaders and teams sharpen their effectiveness, and guide students towards confidence, employability and meaningful careers.

Rooted in defence values, behavioural insight and practical mentoring, our mission is to help individuals think clearly, communicate better, perform under pressure and contribute with purpose — to themselves, their organisations and the nation.

A former Indian Air Force pilot and Qualified Flying Instructor, Group Captain Abhinav Chaturvedi has led, trained and assessed people in high-pressure environments where decisions are time-bound and consequences are real.

Trained in behavioural assessment at the Defence Institute of Psychological Research (DIPR), he brings together operational leadership, psychological insight and practical mentoring to help aspirants, professionals and teams perform with clarity, confidence and responsibility.

CO-FOUNDER

Flight Lieutenant Namrata Chaturvedi (Retd.)

Co-Founder, Alpha Lead Academy · Psychologist & Youth Mentor
Early Women Officer to Fly Aircraft in IAF Psychologist & Counsellor Adolescent & Professional Trainer Youth Leadership Mentor

Flight Lieutenant Namrata Chaturvedi (Retd.), Co-Founder of Alpha Lead Academy, was among the early women officers to fly aircraft in the Indian Air Force. After her service, she moved into psychology, counselling and training, working extensively with adolescents, college students and professionals.

She brings together pioneering experience, initiative, energy and psychological understanding to help individuals build confidence, improve performance and achieve stronger life and career outcomes.
